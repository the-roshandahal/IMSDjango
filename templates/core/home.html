What we do

Six disciplines. One standard.

Whether it's a live platform, a 40-storey lobby or a warehouse floor, the same standard applies: trained crews, the right chemicals, and a record of what was done and when.

Daily

Daily facility cleaning

Floors, amenities, common areas and high-touch surfaces cleaned to a fixed schedule, with consumables and usage tracked per site.

Project

Deep clean & restoration

Shutdowns, handovers and larger works run as scheduled projects -- toolbox talks, attendance and daily hours captured against every crew member on site.

Health

Sanitisation & infection control

Hospital-grade disinfection for outbreak response, food-contact areas and anywhere the standard is higher than "clean".

Specialist

Transport & rail cleaning

Crews work around live services, peak crowds and platform safety rules -- RIW-certified, and briefed for the corridor they're in.

Compliance

Compliance, equipment & fleet

Chemical stock, test-and-tag records and vehicle checks managed centrally, so every site stays audit-ready year round.

Logistics

Waste & consumables management

Stock levels, reordering and waste streams managed so crews never run out mid-shift and nothing goes untracked.

How we run a site

The same four steps, every site, every time

Consistency is the product. This is the sequence that runs behind every contract, large or small.

01

Assess & scope

Site walk-through, risk assessment, and a scope of works agreed before a crew ever arrives.

02

Mobilise & train

Crews rostered, toolbox-talked, and RIW/WHS checked before the first shift starts.

03

Clean

Daily, deep clean or project work delivered to the schedule the site actually needs.

04

Log & audit

Stock, hours and compliance recorded against the site -- every time, no exceptions.
