Inventory Management System

User Guide

Everything you need to run inventory, equipment, vehicles, deep clean projects, and purchasing through the Cleantech1 IMS — on desktop or on your phone.

01 Signing in

  1. Open the IMS login page and enter your username or email and password.
  2. Some accounts require a second step — a 6-digit code. Enter it when asked.
  3. After 10 minutes of no activity you'll be signed out automatically. Just log back in.
Locked out or forgot your password? Only an Administrator can reset your account or unlock it — contact them directly.

02 What your role can do

Every account has one role, set by an Administrator. Your role decides what shows up in your menu and what you're allowed to do — you'll only see the sections described in this guide that your role actually uses.

RoleDay-to-day job
AdministratorFull access to everything, including user management and system-wide settings.
Warehouse SupervisorRuns a warehouse: manage stock, approve station requests, assign equipment/vehicles, create purchase orders, see reports for their warehouse.
Warehouse StaffWorks in a warehouse: view products, view equipment/vehicles assigned to them, take part in stocktakes, create purchase orders.
Station SupervisorRuns a cleaning station: request stock from a warehouse, record daily chemical usage, view reports for their station.
Station Cleaning StaffWorks at a station: request stock, record chemical usage.
Deep Clean SupervisorRuns deep clean projects: dispatch chemicals, take equipment/vehicles, log shifts and hours, close out projects.
ManagementRead-only oversight across the whole company: products, requests, equipment, vehicles, projects, suppliers, purchase orders, reports.

03 Dashboard

The first screen after login:

  • Quick counts — products, warehouses, stations, low stock, pending requests, plus role-specific alerts like equipment maintenance due or expired safety test tags.
  • Low stock alerts — products at or below their reorder point.
  • Recent activity — the latest 20 stock movements, with a "View all" link to the full history.
  • Pending stock requests waiting on approval.
  • Administrators also see a live Audit trail.

Tap any number or row to jump straight to that item.

04 Inventory

Products

Search by name or barcode. Each product shows current stock, its reorder point (the level that triggers a low-stock alert), and a hazard flag if it's a hazardous chemical. Open a product for stock by location, batch/expiry info, its QR code, Safety Data Sheet, and file attachments.

Warehouse Supervisors/Admins create and edit products, and set the reorder point — set it low for slow-moving items, higher for chemicals you burn through fast, so the alert means something for that specific product.

Categories

Simple grouping for products (e.g. "Chemicals", "PPE", "Equipment consumables"). Admins manage these.

Transactions

The full, permanent history of every stock movement — stock in, stock out, transfers, returns, damaged/lost/expired write-offs, station usage. This list can never be edited or deleted, by design — it's the audit trail for where every unit of stock went.

05 Warehouses & stations

  • Warehouses hold bulk stock — each has an address and active/inactive status.
  • Stations are the railway station cleaning sites stock gets issued to.
  • Open either to see current stock levels there.
  • Warehouse Supervisors manage stock in their warehouse (receiving, adjustments, transfers) from the warehouse detail page.

06 Stock requests

How a station gets more stock from a warehouse.

  1. Station staff/supervisor: open the station, click Request stock, pick a warehouse, add products and quantities, submit.
  2. Warehouse Supervisor: sees the request in their queue, Approve or Reject (a rejection needs a reason).
  3. Once approved, the warehouse Dispatches it — deducts warehouse stock and adds it to the station automatically. If there isn't enough stock for everything, it dispatches what's available and marks the rest short (status: Partially Fulfilled).

Filter the list by status: Pending, Approved, Rejected, Partially Fulfilled, Fulfilled.

07 Deep clean projects

For multi-day deep-clean jobs at a station — tracks chemicals, equipment, vehicles, and hours separately from routine station stock.

  • Admin creates the project: reference, name, station, supervisor, start/end dates.
  • Deep Clean Supervisor runs it from the project's detail page:
    • Dispatch chemicals from a warehouse, and Return what's left over when done.
    • Take equipment / Take vehicle — checks it out to the project (blocked if it has overdue maintenance or a failed safety test, unless you tick Override with a reason).
    • Release equipment/vehicles when finished with them.
    • Log a shift — date, day/night, start and end time, who worked it. Hours calculate automatically, including overnight shifts crossing midnight.
    • Close project — only works once everything checked out has been released. If something's outstanding you'll see exactly what; override with a reason if you must close anyway.
A project moves itself from Planned → Active the first time anything happens on it (a dispatch, an equipment take, or a shift log), and to Completed when you close it.

08 Equipment & vehicles

Equipment

Reusable gear (vacuums, scrubbers, pressure washers), each with its own asset ID and QR code. Assign to a station or project (and a specific person if useful), Release when done, Start/End maintenance, or record a safety test result. Equipment with overdue maintenance or a failed/overdue safety test is blocked from assignment — override with a reason if genuinely necessary. Mark lost or Write off when equipment is gone for good (write-off is permanent).

Test tags

For smaller electrical items too numerous to track individually (extension cords, chargers, power leads). Record name, location, start date, expiry date. Filter by expired / expiring soon.

Vehicles

Same idea: assign to a station/project and driver, release, log running costs (fuel, tolls, repairs), track service due dates and insurance expiry. Vehicles with overdue service or expired insurance are blocked from assignment unless overridden.

09 Suppliers & purchase orders

Suppliers

Contact details for who you buy from, plus what products each supplier sells and at what price — used to auto-fill pricing on a purchase order.

Purchase orders

  1. Create: pick a supplier, the warehouse it's delivering to, add line items (product, quantity, unit price — auto-fills from the supplier's saved price if set up).
  2. Starts as a Draft — add or remove lines freely.
  3. Send to supplier locks it and makes it official.
  4. Print gives a clean printable copy.
  5. When stock arrives, Receive it against each line — partial deliveries are fine. Warehouse stock updates the moment you record a receipt.

Status tracks itself: Draft → Sent → Partially Received → Received. Cancel is available any time before it's fully received (needs a reason).

10 Reports

On-screen reports, filterable by date range where relevant:

  • Inventory — on hand, estimated stock value, batch ageing, what's expiring soon.
  • Consumption — what's been issued out, by station, by project, and by product.
  • Equipment — status breakdown, maintenance history, most-assigned items.
  • Deep clean projects — cost per project, on-time status, outstanding assets.
  • Purchasing — spend by supplier, average lead time, on-time delivery rate.
  • Vehicles — running costs, service/insurance compliance issues.
  • Audit summary — activity volume by user and action (Admin/Management only).

What you see is scoped to your role — a Warehouse Supervisor sees their own warehouse's numbers, Deep Clean Supervisors see their own projects, Admin and Management see everything.

Reports are on-screen only for now — no PDF/Excel export or scheduled email reports yet.

11 Notifications

Tap Notifications (top-right, with an unread count badge) for your alerts:

  • Low stock — a product you're responsible for dropped to/below its reorder point.
  • Expiring stock — a batch is expiring soon or has expired.
  • Maintenance due — equipment, a test tag, or a vehicle needs attention.
  • Outstanding request — a stock request has been sitting too long unactioned.
  • Purchase order — an order was fully received, or is overdue against its expected delivery date.

You also get an email for each one, if your account has an email address on file. Tap Open to jump straight to what it's about, or Mark all read to clear the badge.

12 Attaching files

Products, purchase orders, equipment, vehicles, and deep clean projects each have an Attachments section on their detail page — for Safety Data Sheets, certificates, invoices, service records, contracts, site photos.

  • Accepted types: PDF, JPG, PNG, DOCX, XLSX, up to 10MB each.
  • Tap Upload, choose the file, add an optional description.
  • Anyone who can edit that record can also upload or remove its attachments.

13 Administration — Admin only

Users

Create accounts, assign roles, assign which warehouses/stations a user covers, deactivate accounts (with a reason — logged), reset passwords, unlock locked-out accounts.

Audit log

A permanent, unchangeable record of every significant action — logins, role changes, every inventory movement, equipment/vehicle status changes. Nothing here can ever be edited or deleted, by anyone, including Administrators.

14 Using it on your phone

  • Tap the ☰ icon top-left to open the menu; tap outside it or a menu item to close it.
  • Wide tables scroll sideways within the table itself — swipe left/right on the table, not the whole page.
  • Forms and buttons are sized for tapping — no need to zoom in.
  • Everything works the same as on desktop; only the layout adapts.

15 Common questions

I don't see a menu item I need.
Your role doesn't have access to it. Ask your Administrator if you think that's wrong.
A button or action is greyed out or missing.
Usually a permission or status issue (e.g. you can't dispatch a stock request until it's approved). Check the item's status first.
Equipment/vehicle assignment is blocked.
It has overdue maintenance, an overdue/failed safety test, or (for vehicles) overdue service/expired insurance. Fix the underlying issue, or override with a reason if it's genuinely urgent — overrides are logged.
I closed a project by mistake, or need to undo something.
Nothing in this system can be deleted or undone by design — it's an audit trail. Contact your Administrator to work out the right correction.
Where do I report a bug or ask for a new feature?
Contact your Administrator.
